## Organization

Each paper has its own directory named `YEAR-first-author-short-topic`. A paper entry should include:

- bibliographic metadata and stable links;
- a short summary of the study design and main result;
- notes on the figures discussed during the meeting;
- open questions, limitations, and possible follow-up ideas;
- a BibTeX citation;
- the article PDF only when its license permits redistribution.

Use [`templates/paper-notes.md`](templates/paper-notes.md) when adding a new paper.

## PDF policy

This repository stores article PDFs only when the publisher or authors provide an open license that permits redistribution. For other papers, include a DOI or publisher link instead of committing the PDF.
